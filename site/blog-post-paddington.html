Beyond Footfall: How AI Video Analytics Is Unlocking Millions in Value at Paddington Square

Paddington Square is one of London’s most ambitious new destinations. We’re proud to share how PercepVision AI, in partnership with CIS Security, has transformed this landmark development.

A Dual-Purpose Destination With Dual Challenges

Our October 2025 case study revealed the unique rhythm of Paddington Square:

  • Weekday Commuter Power: Around 15,000 escalator journeys driven by oﬃce and rail traﬃc.
  • Weekend Leisure Shift: Approximately 13,000 escalator journeys, dominated by retail and leisure visitors.

This clarity enables:

  • Security and Facility Management (FM) teams to staff precisely for peak periods.
  • Leasing teams to justify rental values with transparent, evidence‑based footfall patterns.

Paddington Square isn’t just busy - it’s predictably busy, and that predictability is commercially powerful.

From Data to Revenue: Turning Insights Into Strategy

The real commercial uplift comes from PercepVision’s granular Occupancy Insights, which reveal how people actually use the space.

Key Findings & Strategic Opportunities

Zone Finding Strategic Opportunity
B1 Ticket Hall Highest occupancy; the primary interchange between rail and retail. A high‑performance zone ideal for premium tenants, targeted activations, and high‑value promotions.
Concourse (Escalator 4) Least utilised; lower relevance in main circulation routes. A growth zone perfect for experiential retail, events, and interventions designed to unlock untapped value.

These insights are already helping the estate team:

  • Validate the worth of high-traffic areas
  • Identify underperforming spaces
  • Convert low‑engagement zones into new revenue opportunities

This is commercial strategy powered by real‑world behaviour, not assumptions.
